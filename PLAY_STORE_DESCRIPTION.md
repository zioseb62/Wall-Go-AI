# Wall Go AI - Google Play Store Assets

## Full Description (English)

Step into the neon arena and prove your tactical superiority in **Wall Go AI**!

Wall Go AI is a fast-paced, grid-based strategy board game that blends classic territory-capture mechanics with a modern, cyberpunk aesthetic and diverse environments. Face off against sophisticated AI opponents in intense battles for 2, 3, or 4 players.

**THE OBJECTIVE**
Move your pieces across the grid to isolate and enclose portions of the board. Every cell you surround becomes your territory, earning you points. The player with the most territory when the board is fully claimed wins the game!

**NEW ENVIRONMENTS**
Choose your battlefield! From the high-contrast **Neon Grid** to the lush **Islands** and the ancient **Desert Ziggurat**, each theme offers a unique visual experience and tactical layout.

**THE WALLBREAKER**
Feeling trapped? Use your limited-use **Wallbreaker** to smash through enemy defenses and reclaim your freedom. But use it wisely—once it's gone, you'll need to rely on your wits (or an opponent's mistake) to escape!

**SMART GAMEPLAY**
*   **Adaptive AI**: Challenge yourself against Ruby, Emerald, and Amber—AI personalities with distinct difficulty levels.
*   **Auto-Pass Logic**: No more waiting! If a player is completely trapped without a Wallbreaker, the game automatically skips their turn to keep the action flowing.
*   **Instant Victory**: The game intelligently detects when no more moves are possible or all territory is claimed, ending the match immediately for a smooth experience.

**FEATURES:**
*   Stunning Cyberpunk, Island, and Desert themes.
*   Support for 2, 3, or 4 players.
*   Advanced AI powered by tactical engines.
*   Smooth animations and immersive sound effects.
*   Detailed match statistics to track your wins and losses.

Are you ready to outsmart the competition? Download **Wall Go AI** today and claim your territory!
